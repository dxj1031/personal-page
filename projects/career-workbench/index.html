A customisation of someone else's open-source project. What is mine is listed slide by slide.

The problem

The job hunt lived in five tabs and a spreadsheet.

Postings, notes, tailored CVs and follow-ups were spread across job boards, documents and a sheet. career-ops already chains these steps with agents, but it is English-first and command-line-first.

I wanted one Chinese interface for the whole loop, while keeping every application document in English.
